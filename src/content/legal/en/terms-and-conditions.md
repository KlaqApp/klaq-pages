## Moderation

To keep the community healthy, the Application has these safeguards:

- **Automatic filter:** text with banned terms is not published.
- **Posting limit:** up to 5 posts per minute and 100 per day.
- **Reports:** anyone can report a review or reply (spam, harassment, unmarked spoiler or another reason). When 3 different people report the same content, it is hidden until a moderator reviews it.
- **Human review:** moderators review reports and decide whether to keep or remove the content.
- **Blocking:** you can block anyone. A block hides each person's content from the other, both ways.
- **Bans:** people who break the rules seriously or repeatedly may be banned. A banned account can no longer post, and its reviews and replies stop showing to others.

If you think a moderation decision was a mistake, email klaq@gabrielhenrique.dev explaining the case. We reply within a reasonable time, with the reason for the final decision.

Illegal content or content that infringes your rights can also be reported at the same address, with enough information to identify it.
